The problem

Paperwork waited on someone to send it

A deal could reach the right stage and then sit until someone remembered to prepare and send the quote or document.

How it works

From trigger to finished work

Trigger: a CRM entry is created or updated.

01

Record created or updated

Zoho CRM sends the change to the flow as soon as it happens.

02

If/else check

The flow checks the record. Only records that meet the condition continue.

03

Send the document

When the condition is true, Zoho Writer sends the document.

Why it matters for the business

What changes when this runs

Documents go out on time

A quote or document is sent as soon as the deal is ready, not when someone gets to it.

Fewer stalled deals

The next step happens automatically, which keeps the pipeline moving.

Consistent wording

Every document comes from the same Writer template.
